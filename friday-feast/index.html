🦃 The Friday Feast is Back! 🦃

Friday, November 21, 2025

We Stand 4 Something is proud to bring back The Friday Feast — a night of food, fun, and community!
This free celebration is more than just a meal — it’s a space where everyone belongs. People with and without disabilities come together to share joy, connection, and holiday cheer. For many, this is the only holiday celebration where they feel truly welcomed. It’s a night where strangers become friends, families come together, and everyone has a seat at the table.
