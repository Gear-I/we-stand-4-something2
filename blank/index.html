Donate!

We rely heavily on the support of the community! Show your love today - send us a donation - no matter what size! Rest assured your funds will go directly to support our programs and bring someone with a disability one step closer to realizing the dream of living a full, active and rewarding life!
When shopping on Amazon, use Amazon Smile! Each time you shop with Amazon smile, Amazon donates 0.5% of the price of your purchase to your favorite charity, We Stand 4 Something! All that you have to do is log onto smile.amazon.com. On your first visit you will be able to select your charitable organization of choice. Simply put We Stand 4 Something and then shop away!
